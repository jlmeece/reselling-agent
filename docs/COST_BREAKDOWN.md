# 💸 The Reselling Engine · Cost Breakdown

Full transparency on what this costs to run.

**Only Claude bills.** Model `claude-sonnet-4-6`. Everything else runs free.

**The rule:** ~$0.03 per product, end to end. That's the one-time cost to score a product and write its listing.

**Paid calls:**
- Research scoring · ~$0.015 per product
- Listing copy · ~$0.016 per product (5 per call, prompt cached)

**Free ($0), no matter the volume:**
- eBay comp research · discovery · savings scans
- Active monitor · eBay sync · audit · graveyard · reprice
- Spot prices · analytics · the Telegram bot

**Volume estimates (products run through the tool):**
- 0-50 products → $0 to ~$1.50
- 50-100 products → ~$1.50 to ~$3.00
- 100-150+ products → ~$3.00 to ~$4.50, then ~$0.03 each after

**The key thing to know:** cost scales with how many products you feed through, not how many you keep listed. A live listing costs $0 to maintain. Even at 300+ products a month, the bill stays under ~$10.
